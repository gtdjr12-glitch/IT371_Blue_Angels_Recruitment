The purpose of this website is to give enlisted Sailor and Marines a better insight of was the application process for the Blue Angels is like, and what the expectations and roles are after getting hired. The Blue Angels are a unique team that require all team mates to qualify in unique roles that are not used in the Fleet. The process can be very demanding, but at the end, you will earn you Crest and officially become a team member of the United States Navy Blue Angels!
