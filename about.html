About This Site

The purpose of this website is to give enlisted Sailors and Marines a better understanding of what the application process for the Blue Angels is like, as well as the expectations and roles after being selected for the team.

The Blue Angels are a unique team that requires team members to qualify in specialized roles that are not normally used in the Fleet. The process can be demanding, but earning the team crest and officially becoming a member of the United States Navy Blue Angels is a major accomplishment.
