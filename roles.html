Roles On The Team

Enlisted Roles

Enlisted personnel support the Blue Angels through maintenance, supply, logistics, administration, and public affairs roles. Everyone, not matter their rate or MOS, has a place on the flight line to support the Demo. Every team member has to learn the positions of other team members.

These jobs help keep the team organized, prepared, and ready to support the mission.
