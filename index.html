Welcome

This website is designed to educate Sailors and Marines about enlisted opportunities with the Blue Angels. The goal is to explain what different enlisted jobs contribute, how those jobs support the demonstration mission, and how interested service members can begin learning about the process.

Website Purpose

This site will include information about the team, enlisted support roles, general requirements, and helpful resources.

Notice

This site is an unofficial educational project and does not replace official Navy, Marine Corps, or Blue Angels guidance.
